Who We Are

Empowering Youth, Uniting Communities

Roots Initiative for Social Empowerment (RISE) is a youth-led, community-driven, and impact-focused Community-Based Organization operating in Kakuma Refugee Camp and Sorrounding Host Communities in Turkana West Sub-County.
Founded on the principle of local self-reliance and unity, RISE brings together young people from diverse refugee and host communities to tackle shared challenges, cultivate untapped talents, and build lasting peace.
Through our work model and community-first approach, we bridge social divides, foster economic self-sufficiency, and create safe, inclusive spaces where every young person can thrive.

Our Vision

A resilient, peaceful, and self-reliant community where every young person is empowered through Sports, Arts, Leadership and Economic opportunities to reach their full potential, preserve their heritage, and lead sustainable community transformation.

Long-Term Impact

Our Mission

To foster social cohesion, holistic well-being, and economic self-sufficiency among refugee and host community youth through integrated peacebuilding, sports development, creative arts, leadership development, and substance rehabilitation programs.

Programmatic Architecture

4 Strategic Pillars

Our programmatic activities are structured around four strategic thematic pillars for sustainable impact.

Pillar 1

Peacebuilding, Governance and Social Cohesion

Fostering conflict resolution, leadership, and institutional dialogues between refugee communities and the hosts.

  • Youth Parliament Sittings
  • Peer Mediation Training
  • Inter-Cultural Dialogue Forums
Pillar 2

Sports, Arts & Talent Development

Promoting youths' participation in sports tournaments under our SporTa framework, traditional athletics, and multi-discipline creative arts.

  • RISE SporTa Holiday Leagues
  • RISE Creative Academy (12 Disciplines)
  • RISE Traditional Sports Tournaments
  • Cultural Festivals & Talent Showcases.
Pillar 3

Youth Empowerment & Well-being

Community addiction recovery, mindfulness, psycho-social support, and dedicated young womens' empowerment initiatives.

  • Substance Rehabilitation Programs
  • Yoga & Trauma Mindfulness Programs
  • Girls & Women in Sports (GWIS)
Pillar 4

Skills, Innovation & Sustainability

Digital literacy, monetization training for creatives, public murals, and environmental action projects.

  • Digital Media Literacy
  • Creative Monetization
  • Public Cohesion Murals
  • Environmental actions
